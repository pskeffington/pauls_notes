## Verification status and limits

Source URLs and general topical applicability were inspected during the October 2026 reference pass. This does not establish continuous link availability, currentness of every guideline, claim-level scientific peer review, or independent replication. The citation validator checks mapped section references and bibliography integrity; specialist review and original-source verification are required before release. All numeric exercises in the current manuscript use fabricated teaching data.
